# Carrom Multiplayer Game

this is  a simple two player Carrom game made for the browser....

Player 1 plays with white coins 
and Player 2 plays with black coins
Place the striker on your line, pull it back, and release to take a shot.

### NOTE - PLEASE PLAY AT 50% SCREEN ZOOM OUT, IT MAY BE NOT SCREEN FRIENDLY

## Features

- it is made for local 2 player
- it have real carrom physics
- White and black coins
- Queen and queen cover rule
- Striker fouls  ( - 1 )
- Extra turn when you pocket your own coin
- Live score and pocketed coin display
- Shot.. collision...pocket and foul sounds
- Restart match option


## How to Play

1. Move the striker along your baseline.
and to move striker here and there click on the space where you want to move
2. Click and drag away from the striker to aim and choose power.
3. Release to shoot.
4. Pocket your own coloured coins to score.
5. Pocketing your own coin gives you another turn.
6. The queen must be covered with one of your own coins.
7. Fouls can return one of your pocketed coins to the board.

The player with the higher score at the end wins.
